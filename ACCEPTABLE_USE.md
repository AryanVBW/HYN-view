**Effective date and last updated: 21 August 2026**

**Version: 1.0**

This policy forms part of the HYN-view [Terms of Use](TERMS.md) for the Hosted
Service operated by **NEXUSV TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED**. It also states the
uses of the open-source agent that NexusV supports. Copyright permission under

## 1. Required authorisation

Use HYN-view only on systems that you own or have express authority to
administer. Follow all laws, workplace rules, contracts and hosting-provider
policies that apply. Give notices and obtain permissions required for monitoring
employees, contractors, customers or other users.

## 2. Prohibited conduct

Do not use HYN-view or the Hosted Service to:

- access, link, scan or monitor any machine, account, network or tenant without
  authority;
- develop, deploy, conceal or support malware, ransomware, botnets, credential
  theft, data exfiltration, phishing, exploitation or persistence;
- stalk, covertly surveil, harass, intimidate or profile a person;
- collect passwords, private keys, authentication tokens, message contents or
  other data that is not necessary for authorised server-health monitoring;
- access another customer's data or evade authentication, tenant isolation,
  rate limits, quotas, suspension or other security controls;
- overload, disrupt, scrape, benchmark or security-test the Hosted Service
  without prior written permission;
- upload malicious input, falsified telemetry or content intended to exploit a
  dashboard, log processor, notification destination or another user;
- send spam, abusive, deceptive or unlawful messages through notification
  features;
- infringe privacy, intellectual-property or other rights, or violate sanctions,
  export controls or other applicable law; or
- represent that NexusV endorses, certifies or guarantees your system or use.

Do not rely on HYN-view as the sole control for a safety-critical or high-risk
system. Use independent monitoring and qualified human administration.

## 3. Security research

Test only accounts and machines you own, unless you have written permission
that specifically covers the test. Do not access or retain another person's
data, impair availability, send unsolicited traffic, use social engineering or
publish a vulnerability before NexusV has had a reasonable opportunity to
investigate. Report findings privately under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy does not create a bug-bounty programme, promise payment or grant
permission to violate law or a third party's terms.

## 4. Enforcement

NexusV may investigate suspected violations and may rate-limit, pause, suspend,
revoke or terminate Hosted Service access. Immediate action may be taken where
reasonably necessary to protect users, providers, the public or the service, or
to comply with law. Where appropriate and lawful, NexusV will give notice and a
reason.
