# Hosted Service Providers and Subprocessors

**Last updated: 21 August 2026**

The HYN-view Hosted Service operated by **NEXUSV TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED**
uses the following primary providers. A provider receives only the categories
needed for its function. Provider regions and practices can change under the
provider's own terms.

| Provider | Function | Information that may be processed | Location note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Vercel** | Hosts and delivers the web application; request and security processing | IP address, request metadata, application responses and operational logs | Distributed/global infrastructure; not limited to India |
| **Supabase** | Database, API and account authentication | Account and auth records, server configuration, telemetry, notification records and administrative records | Primary database region configured as Mumbai, India; support, logs or backups may involve other locations under Supabase terms |
| **Resend** | Hosted Service transactional email | Recipient email address, message subject and body, delivery and error metadata | Provider infrastructure may process data outside India |

**Google** is involved only when Google sign-in is enabled and selected. Google
then authenticates the user and returns account identity information such as an
email address; HYN-view does not receive the Google password.

An administrator can configure other destinations or endpoints, including
SMTP, Brevo, Telegram, ntfy, Slack, Discord, webhooks, public-IP services,
latency and DNS targets, throughput-test endpoints, update registries and
dead-man's-switch services. Those are customer-directed recipients or endpoints
contacted directly from the monitored server and are not necessarily NexusV
subprocessors. Their destinations and credentials are configured locally, not
stored in the Hosted Service. The administrator is responsible for selecting
them and reviewing their terms, security, regions and data use.

Where EU/EEA transfer restrictions apply, an appropriate transfer mechanism and
supplementary safeguards must apply before a restricted transfer. Contact
<vivek.aryanvbw@gmail.com> for current provider information or business
data-processing terms.

NexusV will update this list when a primary provider changes and, where
practical, give Hosted Service customers at least 30 days' notice before adding
a new primary subprocessor that materially changes processing. Urgent security
or continuity changes may occur sooner with notice as soon as reasonably
possible.
